Rules of the Diary

Before you read or post, please agree to the rules.

  • Be kind. No hate, harassment, or targeting.
  • Protect privacy: no full names, numbers, or socials.
  • Keep it heartfelt, not graphic.
  • Write about your feelings, not exposing others.
  • Keep it short and readable (under 200 chars).
  • If it feels unsafe, don't post it.
